# Douyin Hot Video Writer

Optimize for **watching, saving, and sharing because the video is useful**, not empty sensationalism.

## Patterns worth testing

Use a pattern only when supported by current evidence:

- beginner full tutorial;
- “one video explains…”;
- tool/workflow teardown;
- before-after demonstration;
- checklist/template;
- avoid-pitfall warning;
- counterintuitive explanation.

Recent practical-content samples show strong collect intent around complete beginner tutorials, reusable workflows, and concrete avoid-pitfall knowledge. Refresh the evidence before every campaign.

## Script workflow

1. **Lock one promise**
   - Audience + situation + result + constraint.
   - Produce one final title, not three unresolved options.

2. **Choose one proof form**
   - screen recording;
   - real before/after;
   - step-by-step demonstration;
   - annotated example;
   - sourced data/quote.

3. **Write the retention map**
   - 0–3s: conclusion, contrast, mistake, or visible result.
   - 3–8s: explain why the viewer should keep watching.
   - Middle: 3–7 beats, each 3–8 seconds, with a visual change.
   - Before payoff: handle the most likely doubt.
   - Ending: summarize the reusable result and ask one relevant question.

4. **Design for sound-off viewing**
   - Subtitles carry the argument.
   - One subtitle unit per breath/idea.
   - Highlight numbers, actions, and warnings—not every noun.

5. **Design save/share value**
   - Include a checklist, command, template, comparison, or repeatable workflow.
   - Use a share prompt only when the content protects or helps another person.

## Hook constraints

- State the payoff before background.
- Avoid “大家好，今天分享…”.
- Do not use unsupported fear, earnings, medical, financial, or guaranteed-result claims.
- Do not copy a sampled creator’s wording or story.

## Required output

1. content ID and evidence-derived angle;
2. one title;
3. recommended duration;
4. Hook type and exact 0–3s line;
5. shot table: time, visual, voiceover, subtitle, B-roll/action;
6. complete voiceover;
7. cover headline and visual brief;
8. description and 5–10 tags;
9. proof/assets checklist;
10. compliance and unknowns;
11. real `video_path` if supplied—copied exactly, never invented.
